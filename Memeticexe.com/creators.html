royalty tiers — meme execution levels

memetic.exe shares revenue on every qualified design. As designs spread, they level up.

Tier Status Payout (per shirt) Signal
Level 1 New Drop $1 / shirt fresh payload
Level 2 Fan Favorite $2 / shirt community approved
Level 3 Certified Meme Classic $3 / shirt culture locked
Bonus logic:
• Extra rewards if a post featuring your design hits 1,000+ likes.
• Higher payout bands are possible for exclusive collabs (designing only for memetic.exe).

printing blanks — your canvas

We currently print on a focused set of blanks to keep quality tight and colors on-brand:
  • Tees: black, white, light gray, soft blue, muted pink, pale yellow
  • Hoodies: black, soft gray, ice blue, light sage, dusty pink
When you submit a design, you can suggest preferred blank colors — we’ll match art to the best canvas.

roadmap — creator dashboard (phase 2)

As the .exe scales, we plan to ship a lightweight creator dashboard showing:
